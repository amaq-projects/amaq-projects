# Hi, I'm Ali! 👋

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from the **University of Texas at Dallas**, with interests in mechanical design, manufacturing, and engineering analysis.

What I enjoy most about engineering is taking an idea beyond its initial design, figuring out how it works, how it can be manufactured, and where it can be improved. My experience so far has given me opportunities to work on everything from industrial electrical enclosures to an electromechanical tooling fixture developed for my senior capstone.

I created this GitHub to share and document selected engineering projects from my academic and professional experience, along with new projects I take on. I'll continue adding to this portfolio as I revisit past work and explore new ideas.

## 🔧 Featured Project

### [Tooling Fixture FEA & Design Optimization](https://github.com/amaq-projects/Tooling-Fixture-FEA-Optimization)

An FEA and design optimization study of a component from my senior capstone project. Using **Abaqus and MATLAB**, I evaluated its structural performance and explored opportunities for mass reduction while accounting for stress, displacement, and geometric constraints.
